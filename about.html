Intel

I am a final-year AI and Data Science undergraduate building retrieval and multi-agent systems that can be trusted in production — and measured when they are not.

I approach AI engineering with a builder's mindset: start simple, iterate quickly, and measure what matters. My work centres on verification-first RAG architectures and multi-agent workflows, with evaluation treated as a first-class part of the system rather than an afterthought.

Lately that has meant asking harder questions of my own systems: can a retrieval pipeline abstain instead of guessing, can it survive a prompt-injection attempt, and is an LLM actually the right tool for a structured prediction problem — or just the fashionable one?
