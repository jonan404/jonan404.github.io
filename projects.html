Project 02

Customer Churn Prediction & LLM Cost-Accuracy Benchmark

A churn classifier (Random Forest, XGBoost) on 7,000+ telecom accounts with leak-proof preprocessing and class-imbalance handling — then benchmarked head-to-head against zero-shot and few-shot LLM prompting on identical data. The comparison surfaced a numeric-anchoring bias in few-shot outputs and a hidden reasoning-token cost, and turned an AI-hype question into an evidence-based recommendation.
